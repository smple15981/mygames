## Asset Intake Rules

1. Store each imported pack in its own directory.
2. Preserve the original license/readme files supplied with the pack.
3. Record the exact pack version or download date where possible.
4. Do not import assets with unclear origin or redistribution restrictions.
5. Keep only files that are used by the project when repository size becomes a concern.
6. Re-audit every third-party asset before a commercial release.
